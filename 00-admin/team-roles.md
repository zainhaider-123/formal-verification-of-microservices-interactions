# Team Roles and Responsibilities

## Review Team

| Role | Name | Affiliation | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviewer 1 (R1) / Guarantor | Zain Haider | NED University | Independent screening, extraction, risk of bias; overall coordination and final decisions |
| Reviewer 2 (R2) | Ashok Kumar | NED University | Independent screening, extraction, risk of bias |

## Decision Authority

- **Inclusion/exclusion conflicts:** Discussed jointly by R1 and R2; unresolved cases decided by mutual discussion.
- **Protocol amendments:** Require agreement of both reviewers, with a dated entry in the decision log.
- **Risk of bias disagreements:** Resolved through discussion between the two reviewers.

## PRISMA 2020 alignment

This review follows PRISMA 2020 (Page et al., BMJ 2021) for reporting and
PRISMA-P 2015 (Moher et al., Syst Rev 2015) for the protocol structure.
