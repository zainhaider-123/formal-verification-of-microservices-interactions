# Replication Package

Materials needed to replicate or build on this systematic review. Per PRISMA
2020 item 27, this package will be deposited on Zenodo with a DOI before
manuscript submission.

## Contents (planned)

- `S1-search-strings/` — Full search strings for all 6 databases
- `S2-prisma-checklist.pdf` — Completed PRISMA 2020 27-item checklist
- `S3-excluded-studies.xlsx` — Studies excluded at full-text with reasons (item 16b)
- `S4-risk-of-bias-details.xlsx` — Per-study, per-domain risk of bias
- `S5-data-extraction.xlsx` — Complete data extraction from all 23 studies
- `S6-included-studies.bib` — BibTeX of all included studies

## Why a replication package matters

PRISMA 2020 item 27 requires reporting which materials are publicly
available. Reviewers and future researchers should be able to:
1. Verify any number reported in the manuscript
2. Re-run the search to extend the review
3. Apply our extraction form and risk of bias instrument to their own topic
