# Research Questions

**Topic:** Formal Verification of Microservice Interactions

## PCC (Population, Concept, Context) Mapping

This review uses the PCC framework, which is appropriate for
methodological/architectural reviews where the aim is to map and synthesize
approaches rather than compare clinical interventions.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Population (P)** | Microservice-based software systems and the interactions between their microservices |
| **Concept (C)** | Formal verification of microservice interactions: modelling approaches, verified properties, verification techniques and tools, and reported scalability problems and limitations |
| **Context (C)** | Academic and industrial research on microservice systems, across any application domain |

## Research Questions

**RQ1.** Which formal modelling approaches are used to represent interactions between microservices?

**RQ2.** Which properties of microservice interactions are formally verified?

**RQ3.** Which formal verification techniques and tools are used for verifying microservice interactions?

**RQ4.** What scalability problems and limitations are reported when formally verifying microservice interactions?

## Justification of question framework choice

PCC (rather than PICO) is used because this is a mapping-type review of
modelling and verification approaches rather than an intervention-
effectiveness review. Primary studies describe formal approaches and their
application rather than comparing interventions across comparable
populations. This follows PRISMA 2020 item 4, which permits alternative
frameworks where appropriate.
