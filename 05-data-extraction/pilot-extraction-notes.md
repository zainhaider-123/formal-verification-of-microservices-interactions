# Pilot Extraction Notes

## Pilot conducted: 2026-06-23

**Studies piloted:** S001, S002, S003, S007, S010 (selected to span single-case,
multi-case, longitudinal, and mining study designs)

**Reviewers:** R1 and R2 independently extracted each study, then compared
results in a 120-minute session.

## Issues identified during pilot

### Issue 1: Study type taxonomy
**Problem:** The mining study (S010) and the longitudinal retrospective (S007)
didn't fit neatly into the original "Case study / Survey / Experiment" taxonomy.
**Resolution:** Expanded the Study Type controlled vocabulary to include:
- Single case study
- Multi-case study
- Retrospective case study
- Longitudinal retrospective
- Mining study
- Mixed-methods
- Design science + evaluation
- Interview study

### Issue 2: Motivation granularity
**Problem:** Studies often mix primary and secondary motivations. Original
form had only one "Motivation" field.
**Resolution:** Split into "Primary Motivation" and "Secondary Motivations"
with free-text "Operational Pain Points" and "Cost Drivers" for detail.

### Issue 3: Before/after architecture specifications
**Problem:** The number of services "before" and "after" is sometimes a
range or not well-defined.
**Resolution:** Allow ranges ("14-67"), "variable", or "—" in these
columns; detail in notes.

### Issue 4: Team size ambiguity
**Problem:** Is team size the engineering team that performed consolidation,
the whole product team, or the whole organization?
**Resolution:** Defined "Team Size" as the engineering team directly
involved in the consolidation effort. Documented in the form legend.

### Issue 5: Verbatim quotes for findings
**Problem:** Reviewers paraphrased the studies' self-reported findings,
which sometimes changed meaning.
**Resolution:** Added "Verbatim quotes" column requiring at least one
direct quote per study per RQ.

## Form changes after pilot

Extraction form template updated 2026-06-25. The 5 piloted studies were
re-extracted using the new form.

## Time per study (pilot)

- S001: 45 minutes
- S002: 78 minutes (5 cases to extract)
- S003: 52 minutes (long retrospective analysis)
- S007: 67 minutes (10-year history)
- S010: 41 minutes (mining study, less textual detail)

**Average:** 57 minutes per study. Estimated total for 23 studies: ~22 hours.
